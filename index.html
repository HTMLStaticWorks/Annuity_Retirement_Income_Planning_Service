❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear Answers for Confident Planning

Everything you need to know about fixed annuities, rate locks, and retirement cash flow.

How do fixed annuities protect my principal during market downturns?
Fixed MYGA and indexed annuities guarantee your principal capital against stock market losses. Your money is backed by the legal reserves of A++ rated insurance carriers, ensuring that even during severe market drops, your balance and income floor remain 100% secure.
What is the difference between a Fixed MYGA and an Indexed Annuity?
A Fixed Multi-Year Guaranteed Annuity (MYGA) acts like a high-yield CD, offering a set guaranteed interest rate (e.g. 5.65% APY) for 3 to 10 years. An Indexed Annuity ties growth potential to a market index (like the S&P 500) while guaranteeing a 0% floor so you never lose capital when the market drops.
Can I roll over an existing 401(k) or IRA into a Retira annuity plan?
Yes! You can perform a direct trustee-to-trustee 1035 exchange or IRA rollover without incurring any tax penalties or triggering taxable income events. Our advisory team assists with all transfer paperwork.
What happens to my annuity income stream if I pass away?
All annuity contracts include designated primary and contingent beneficiary protections. Remaining contract balances or guaranteed lifetime joint riders pass directly to your spouse or heirs without going through probate court.
